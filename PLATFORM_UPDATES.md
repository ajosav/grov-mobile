# PLATFORM_UPDATES.md

The register of store, OS, SDK and language changes that affect this app: what changed, when it bites, whether we are ready, and the evidence. It is how updates get captured instead of discovered at upload time.

## Rules

- **Add a row the moment you learn of a platform change**, even if it is unverified. Never delete a row; change its status.
- **Evidence column:** a primary source (Apple, Google, Flutter, Dart) or `UNVERIFIED (secondary: <where>)`. Blog posts and AI summaries are secondary: confirm on the primary page before acting or before marking `Done`.
- **Status:** `Done` (implemented and, where checkable, covered by a test) · `Action` (we must do something) · `Watch` (announced, not yet actionable or not yet confirmed) · `N/A` (read, does not apply, say why).
- **Checkable requirements get a test** in `test/platform_compliance_test.dart` so they cannot silently regress.
- **Review cadence:** before every release, on every Flutter/Dart/Xcode bump, and at least monthly. Update "Last reviewed".
- Exact deadline days are often unpublished. Record what the source says ("April 2027"), not a guessed day.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-21 · **Toolchain pinned today:** Flutter 3.50.1, Dart 3.14.0, Xcode 26.6 (macOS 27.0.1)

| AND-007 | **Zero-tap sign-in restoration**: apps that support sign-in (optional or mandatory) must restore the signed-in state after a device-to-device transfer or cloud-backup restore, using the Restore Credentials API (Credential Manager, Android 9+). Create the restore key at startup and right after sign-in, delete it on sign-out. Regulated apps (the page names financial and healthcare) may request an exemption in Play Console **before the enforcement date**. | April 2027 | **Action: decision needed** | [Play Console requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17492799). Read from Google primary pages by a research agent on 2026-11-21; confirm on the page before acting. Affects the sign-on we just built: tokens live in `flutter_secure_storage` under a Keystore key that does not move to a new device (an unreadable store is treated as signed out), so today a new phone means signing in again. Needs (1) a backend way to mint and redeem a restore key (none in the OpenAPI contract), (2) a plugin or platform channel (a dependency), (3) a decision on whether we are a 'regulated' app (we hold savings goals and take payments) and apply for the exemption. **Research 2026-11-21 (Google's guide, read by a research agent; confirm on the page):** the mechanism is **WebAuthn**, not an opaque token. The backend must act as a WebAuthn relying party: issue a challenge, accept and verify a registration (the app calls `createCredential` with the server's `PublicKeyCredentialCreationOptionsJSON`), and later verify an assertion from an **unauthenticated** restore endpoint (`allowCredentials: []`, user verification discouraged, user found by `userHandle` and `credentialId`), then issue a session. The docs do not say a plain server token is acceptable. Needs Android 9+, Google Play services 24.22+, `androidx.credentials` 1.5+; without Play services, fall back to normal sign-in. Create at startup (if signed in and not yet synced) and after sign-in; delete on sign-out and on a 401. Backup: `allowBackup="true"` with a `backupAgent` restores natively before the app opens; `allowBackup="false"` restores on first launch (Dart can do it). Exclude the sync flag in `data_extraction_rules.xml`. Flutter plugins exist but are tiny and new (`android_restore_credentials` 0.1.0, 193 downloads; `zero_tap_easy` 0.1.1); Google gives no Flutter guidance. Exemption: regulated apps (financial, healthcare) 'may be eligible' and must request **before** the enforcement date, but Google says guidance on exceptions comes later, so the process and deadline are unknown. Decision pending: DECISIONS M-039. **Backend today (read from `grov-be` code, 2026-11-21):** no WebAuthn code or library (only `jose`). It has *device keys for step-up*: `POST /v1/devices` stores a P-256 or Ed25519 public key (base64 SPKI) on the signed-in session's device, once, within 10 minutes of sign-in (`DEVICE_KEY_LOCKED` after); `POST /v1/auth/step-up/challenge` returns a random nonce and `POST /v1/auth/step-up` checks a **raw signature over that nonce** and returns a scoped grant. Both need a signed-in session. That is a different mechanism from what Restore Credentials needs (WebAuthn registration plus an unauthenticated assertion that mints a session). **Documented in Grov's own docs 2026-11-21:** decision **D67** (`requirements/design/docs/00-context-and-decisions.md`) and the full design in `requirements/design/docs/10-auth-architecture.md` **§21** (requirement, today versus needed, proposed backend and app design, open questions R1 to R8). |
